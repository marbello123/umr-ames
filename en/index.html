About

A unit serving environment and health

UMR AMES brings together researchers and faculty members from the University of Nouakchott, ENSG, IMROP, ONISPA and PNBA, in accordance with decree No. 001045 governing joint research units.

Mission

To design and apply mathematical, statistical and artificial intelligence methods to model the environmental and health challenges of Mauritania.

Objectives

  • Strengthen the understanding of processes related to the environment and natural resource management.
  • Support decision-making on critical public health and epidemiology issues.
  • Provide predictive and decision-support tools adapted to local contexts.

Research areas

Four interdisciplinary areas

The unit's work focuses on modelling environmental and health systems, at the interface of applied mathematics and life sciences.

💧

Environment & natural resources

Modelling of pollutant transport, groundwater dynamics, and sustainable management of water resources and ecosystems.

🩺

Public health & epidemiology

Models of the spread of infectious and nosocomial diseases, and public health decision support.

📐

Mathematical modelling

Partial differential equations, dynamical systems and numerical methods for porous media and complex phenomena.

🤖

Statistics & artificial intelligence

Machine learning, functional data analysis, and signal and image processing applied to environmental and health sciences.
